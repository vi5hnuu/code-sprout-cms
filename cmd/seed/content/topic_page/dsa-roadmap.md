Data structures and algorithms are easier to learn in the right order: each stage reuses the ideas of the one before. Work through the stages below, read the article for each topic, then solve its practice problems before moving on.

:::callout[How long will it take?]{kind=info}
At about an hour a day, most people get through stages 1–4 in six to eight weeks. Depth matters more than speed. Re-solving a problem a few days later teaches more than racing through new ones.
:::
